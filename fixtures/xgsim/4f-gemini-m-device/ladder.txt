; Existing 4층_GEMINI logic. No diagnostic P rung is added.
; M00001 = START_REQUEST (rising edge), M00002 = STOP_REQUEST (active high)
; M00100 = RUN_LATCH. The physical P03/P02/P21 training slice is bridged to these M devices.

LOADP M00001
AND NOT M00101
SET M00100

LOAD M00002
OR M00103
RST M00100

; Safe disconnect writes M00001=0 and M00002=1, verifies M00100=0, then releases XimUtil.
